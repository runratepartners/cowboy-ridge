Last updated November 24, 2026

This policy explains how Cowboy Ridge Landscape & Design ("Cowboy Ridge Landscape," "we," "us"), based in Middleton, Idaho, collects and uses your information.

What we collect

When you call, text, email, use our website chat or book with us, we collect what you give us: your name, phone number, email, property address and details about the work you want. Our website may also collect basic usage data, like pages visited and device type.

How we use it

To respond to your request, give you estimates, schedule and complete work, send appointment reminders and invoices, ask for feedback, and, if you agree, send occasional seasonal service reminders and offers.

Text messaging

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile phone number or text messaging consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We may share information with service providers that help us run our business (for example, our scheduling, messaging and payment software), only so they can provide those services to us.

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. See our SMS Terms.

How we protect it

We keep your information in secure business software and only use it to serve you. We keep it as long as we need it to provide service and meet legal and tax requirements.
