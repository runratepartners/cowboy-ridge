FAQ

Good questions.

How much does a sprinkler blowout cost?

$10 per zone with a $30 minimum. Tell us your zone count when you text and you'll know the price up front.

When should I schedule my spring start-up?

Irrigation water in the Treasure Valley usually comes on around the end of March. Get on the list in February or early March to get an early slot.

When should I blow out my sprinklers in the fall?

Before the first hard freeze, usually October into early November. Waiting too long risks cracked pipes and backflow damage.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. For installs, hardscape and lighting we come out, walk the property and give you a written scope and price at no charge.

Do you install outdoor lighting on existing landscapes?

Yes. We add path, accent and patio lighting to yards we didn't build too.

What areas do you serve?

Middleton, Star, Caldwell, Eagle, Meridian and Nampa. Larger projects in Boise on request.
