SMS Terms

Last updated November 24, 2026

These terms cover text messages from Cowboy Ridge Landscape & Design ("Cowboy Ridge Landscape," "we," "us").

What you'll receive

By texting us, using the chat on our website, or giving us your mobile number and agreeing to receive texts, you agree to receive text messages from Cowboy Ridge Landscape about your inquiry, estimates, appointment scheduling and reminders, job updates, invoices and review requests. If you also opt in to marketing, we may send occasional seasonal reminders and offers, such as sprinkler start-up and blowout reminders.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How to stop or get help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You'll get one message confirming you've been unsubscribed, and we won't text you again unless you opt back in. Reply HELP for help, or call (208) 519-2801 or email cowboyridgelandscape@gmail.com.

Consent is not a condition of purchase

You don't have to agree to receive marketing texts to buy any service from us.

Privacy

We never sell or share your mobile number or opt-in consent with third parties for their marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
